## Schemas

Use these templates when adding an education record. Replace placeholders with supported facts. The `---` lines delimit valid YAML frontmatter.

### Education: `resume_data/education/<institution-degree-slug>.md`

```markdown
---
id: unique-education-slug
institution: "Institution Name"
location: "City, ST"
degree: "Degree Name"
field: "Field of Study"
start_date: "YYYY-MM"
end_date: "YYYY-MM" # or "Present"
---
```
